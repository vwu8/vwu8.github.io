About me

Strategy grounded in hands-on engineering.

My career has moved between the server room, the project site and the leadership table. I have led infrastructure activities across Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Australia, Japan and China—from assessing data centre providers and designing customer-owned rack environments to coordinating migrations, technology refreshes and business-critical operations.

I enjoy the entire delivery lifecycle: understanding a business need, evaluating options, selecting and procuring the right equipment, working through dependencies, getting systems installed and tested, and making sure they can be supported reliably afterward.

My approach is practical and vendor-aware, but not vendor-led: start with operational requirements, be clear about who owns each part of the infrastructure, and make decisions that will hold up in production.

My approach to leadership is shaped by both enterprise technology delivery and entrepreneurship. When familiar solutions are not enough, I believe in taking responsibility, working through constraints, evaluating alternatives and staying with the problem until there is a practical way forward.
